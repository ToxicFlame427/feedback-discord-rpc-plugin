Broadcast Rich Presence

Off by default. When on, your song, artist, instrument and score are shared with Discord.

Not showing up on Discord?
  1. Use the Discord desktop app on this computer (not discord.com in a browser) and keep it open.
  2. In Discord: User Settings → Activity Privacy → Share your detected activities must be on.
  3. Fee[dB]ack must run directly on this computer — not in Docker or on a NAS.
  4. Your status updates within a few seconds of changing screens or starting a song.
Advanced: use your own Discord app

For forks or custom branding. Create an app in the Discord Developer Portal, upload Rich Presence art assets named app_logo, guitar_icon, bass_icon, drums_icon, keys_icon and vocals_icon, then paste its Application ID here.
